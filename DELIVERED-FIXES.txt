Souqi launch-ready package fixes applied (2026-04-01)

1) Listing sale completion
- Accepting an offer from the seller now marks the listing as sold immediately.
- Other open offers on the same listing are closed automatically.
- Manual mark-sold also closes pending/countered offers.
- Conversation messages and notifications reflect the completed sale.

2) Mandatory listing images
- Creating a new listing now requires at least one image in both app UI and backend.
- App shows clear note that photos are required before publishing.

3) Strict email/phone validation
- Email fields now accept email format only.
- Phone / WhatsApp fields now accept digits only and validate on app + backend.
- Profile, register, OTP, login, and listing forms were tightened.

4) Better sale flow inside the app
- Conversation screen now shows offers with seller actions.
- Seller can accept/reject offer directly from the conversation.
- Once accepted, listing status becomes sold and further negotiation is blocked.
- Sold listings show a clear sold banner and disable new negotiation.

5) Launch cleanliness
- App login fields are blank by default.
- Launch-ready seed remains without sample users/listings/conversations.
